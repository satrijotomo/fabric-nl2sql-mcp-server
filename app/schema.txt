Tables:
sales_orders(order_id, customer_id, order_date, total_amount, region)
customers(customer_id, customer_name, city, country, segment)
products(product_id, product_name, category, subcategory)
order_items(order_id, product_id, quantity, line_amount)

Relationships:
sales_orders.customer_id -> customers.customer_id
order_items.order_id -> sales_orders.order_id
order_items.product_id -> products.product_id

Business rules:
- Revenue means SUM(line_amount)
- Use TOP for row limiting
- Prefer explicit column lists, never SELECT *
- Only read operations are allowed
